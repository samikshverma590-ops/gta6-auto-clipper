# Viral Trend Researcher (Phase 1)

## Objective
Continuously keep `memory/viral_playbook.json` grounded in live GTA 6 short-form algorithm winners across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts—at **$0 cost** and with **zero video downloads**.

## Research Protocol
1. **Scan Top 15 Weekly Winners:** Search public trend trackers (e.g. Virlo, YouTube Shorts search, TikTok Creative Center, gaming press roundups like IGN / Indy100 / Streams Charts) for GTA 6 clips from the last 7 days.
2. **Decode 0–3s Hook Mechanics:**
   - **Visual shock / pattern interrupt:** First-frame Leonida/Vice Beach realism, sudden NPC reaction, mutant crab / car-boot snake jump scare, or split-screen streamer shock face.
   - **Mid-sentence / cold-open start:** Never begin with "Hey guys" or silence; start directly on the punchline, scream, or impossible mechanic.
   - **Curiosity-gap 3-word top banner:** High-contrast 3-word opener (`WAIT FOR IT`, `ROCKSTAR HID THIS`, `NPC DID WHAT?!`, `PS6 GRAPHICS?!`).
3. **Pacing & Loop Engineering:**
   - Cut interval target: **2.0–4.0s** visual/zoom rhythm.
   - Optimal durations: **18–32s** for fast gameplay shocks/Easter eggs; **35–52s** for reaction/story moments.
   - Audio/visual loop: End on a rising beat or unresolved beat that feeds back into frame 0.
4. **Rockstar Video Policy Compliance (Mandatory Guardrail):**
   - Never publish isolated unedited Rockstar cutscenes, ending/major story spoilers, or pre-release leaked footage.
   - Require transformative framing (hook banner, word-by-word captions, streamer reaction stack, or analytical commentary).
5. **Output:** Update `memory/viral_playbook.json` with updated `hook_archetypes`, `winning_topics`, `platform_metadata_templates`, and `scoring_weights`.
